About us

A School Built Around Strong Foundations

Oxford English School, Bhokar, District Nanded, Maharashtra, was founded in 2000. It began as a pre-primary school and has grown into a learning community committed to knowledge, discipline and service.

The school offers English-medium education with a dedicated faculty working to meet state curriculum requirements. Learning is supported through personalised student mentorship, strong teacher–student interaction, and a campus designed for both academic work and daily school life.

With more than two decades of educational commitment, Oxford English School continues to focus on foundational learning, character, and opportunities for every child to grow with confidence.

Our Founder

The Vision Behind Oxford English School

A founding vision centred on knowledge, discipline, service to society, and a modern education system for Bhokar.

Founder’s Vision

Oxford English School was founded in 2000 with a vision centred on meaningful education, knowledge, discipline, and service to society.

Founder Late Honorable Mr. Shivanand Sambhajirao Kadam envisioned an institution where education could help students grow in knowledge, character and responsibility — with loyalty to the nation and a commitment to bringing society into the light of knowledge.

The founding message records that the school began as a pre-primary school in 2000, and that every brick and every step of today’s Oxford English School, Bhokar, stands as a witness to that dream.

It was his appeal to the parents of the school and society for a modern education system — one that the community of Bhokar Taluka could help complete together.

From the founder’s message

Knowledge is Light

The founder’s message affirms a belief that education should impart truthful knowledge, cultivate discipline, and serve society and the nation. Oxford English School continues to honour that archival statement: that knowledge itself is light.
